About
No swords. Only spells. A fantasy 3D action adventure you can jump into instantly — no build homework, just cast, dodge, and push forward. Become a wizard of light and rescue Princess Torosso from the prison island.
No build research. Aim, clear, beat the boss, break through — the flow never stops until the ending.
Projectile and Area spells grow stronger as you stack cards; Summon and Shield trigger the moment you pick them up.
Every chapter ends with an obstacle section that changes how you move — jump, ride, time it right, and reach the next region.
Finish the game and your clear time goes on the Steam leaderboard. Run it again — tighten your route, cut the mistakes, and chase the fastest rescue.

Story
An arrogant imperial prince proposes to Princess Torosso and is rejected. Consumed by pride, he borrows the power of darkness, invades the kingdom, and takes the princess to a cursed prison island.
A young wizard of light, bound to the princess by love, guilt, and duty, crosses the sea alone.
Now it is time to cut off the fate of darkness with your own hands.
