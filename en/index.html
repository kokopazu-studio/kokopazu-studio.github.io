Magic system

Four spell cards. No blade.

Projectile and Area spells grow stronger as you stack cards; Summon and Shield trigger the moment you pick them up.

Story

In a world where the bloodlines of light and darkness still remain.

An arrogant imperial prince proposes to Princess Torosso and is rejected. Consumed by pride, he borrows the power of darkness, invades the kingdom, and takes the princess to a cursed prison island.

A young wizard of light, bound to the princess by love, guilt, and duty, crosses the sea alone.

Now it is time to cut off the fate of darkness with your own hands.

Wizard of light

Haeon

A wandering wizard of the bloodline of light. He fights with a staff and the Dawn Sigil stone — never a sword.

The one to rescue

Princess Torosso

Princess of a small kingdom and heir to the line that guards the seal. In a crisis, she thinks of her people first.

The fallen prince

Dark Knight

The imperial prince who took the power of darkness. What he became, you will find on the prison island.

Studio

Kokopazu Studio

KOKOPAZU STUDIO is a one-person independent game studio built around a simple question: does an adventure game always need complex skill trees and weapon combinations? Its first title, Save Princess Torosso, combines intuitive progression and ranged magic combat with platform traversal — one continuous adventure you can stay immersed in until the ending.
